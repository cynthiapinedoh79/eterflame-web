FAQ

Questions we always get.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A focused branding project typically takes 2–4 weeks. A full web build runs 4–8 weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline after our first conversation.

What is your pricing?

Every project is scoped individually — we don't believe in one-size pricing. Budget range and timeline are part of our briefing form so we can give you an honest, accurate quote from the start.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes. Eterflame works fully remote with clients across North America, Europe, and Latin America. Time zones have never stopped a good project.

What do I need to get started?

Just an idea and a rough sense of budget. You don't need a brief, a deck, or a finished concept — that's what the discovery process is for. Fill out our project form and we'll take it from there.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. We offer post-launch support, maintenance, and retainer arrangements depending on the project. We'd rather stay involved and make sure everything keeps working than hand off and disappear.

Can you work with an existing brand or website?

Absolutely. We work with brands at any stage — from zero to established. Whether you need a full rebuild or refinement of what's already there, we adapt to where you are.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, always. Confidentiality is standard on every project. We're happy to sign your NDA or provide our own before any conversation involving sensitive information.

What makes Eterflame different?

We're a small studio that works like a strategic partner, not a vendor. Every project gets senior-level attention, intentional design, and technical execution that actually holds up. We care about the work — not just the deliverable.
